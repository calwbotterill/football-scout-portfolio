Match Overview

Arsenal U18s were beaten 3-2 by Crystal Palace U18s in an open U18 Premier League fixture at Sobha Realty Training Centre. Arsenal looked to control the game through patient build-up from the goalkeeper, using inverted full-back movements and technical combinations in midfield to progress possession and create overloads between the lines. Although they produced several positive attacking sequences, defensive lapses and vulnerability from set plays proved costly in decisive moments.

Crystal Palace defended in a compact back four out of possession before regularly transitioning into a back three during build-up, with the right-back moving inside as a third centre-back and the left-back advancing high as a wing-back. This structure allowed them to retain possession effectively while creating width on the left. Palace combined composed build-up with quick attacking transitions and strong second-ball play, ultimately proving more clinical in front of goal to secure the victory.
