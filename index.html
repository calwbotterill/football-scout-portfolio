SquadIQ

Turning football analysis into clear decisions

SquadIQ is a scouting and squad management platform I developed from my own analysis workflow. It was built to bring player profiling, match preparation and recruitment assessment into a single system.

The platform reflects how I approach football analysis: structured, evidence-based and focused on supporting better decisions on and off the pitch.

Building SquadIQ has strengthened my understanding of player evaluation, role suitability, squad planning and football intelligence beyond traditional scouting methods.
